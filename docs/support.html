Support for Big4 Watchability

Common questions

Why don't I see a score for a game that hasn't started or is in progress?
Scores are spoiler-safe by design — they only appear once a game reaches its final period, or after it's finished. This is intentional, not a bug.

Why is a game missing a highlights link?
Highlights are matched automatically from official league YouTube channels shortly after a game ends. Not every game gets a match right away — check back later, or use the in-app link option if available for your league.

Can I turn off alerts/notifications?
Yes — go to Settings and disable Alerts, or turn off notifications for the app in your device settings.

My favorite team/player disappeared from Favorites.
Favorites are stored locally on your device. They persist across app updates but are removed if the app is uninstalled or its storage is manually cleared in your device settings.
