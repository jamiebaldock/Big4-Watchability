FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters — currently ~1500)

Big4 Watchability tells you which games are actually worth watching — without spoiling anything.

Every NBA, WNBA, MLB, NFL, and NHL game gets a Watchability Score once it's final, based on what actually makes a game exciting: close margins, big comebacks, lead changes, clutch finishes, buzzer-beaters, overtime, and standout individual performances. The score and every dramatic detail stay hidden until you're ready — no accidental score-checking, no spoiled finishes.

SPOILER-FREE BY DESIGN
- Scores and outcomes are hidden until a game is final (or deep in the closing stages).
- A short, pregame-style preview blurb is generated for every upcoming game — matchup context and storylines, never a hint at how it turns out.
- Once a game ends, tap to reveal a full breakdown: margin, comebacks, lead changes, and more — still never the actual score or winner unless you choose to see it.

BROWSE YOUR WAY
- Swipe through a full week of games, past and future, for any of the five leagues.
- Pull to refresh live scores and game state as they happen.
- Sort games best-first once you're ready to see ratings.

FIVE LEAGUES, ONE APP
Switch between NBA, WNBA, MLB, NFL, and NHL in a tap. NBA Summer League games are included automatically during the offseason.

MAKE THE RATING YOURS
Tune how much each factor — margin, comebacks, clutch finishes, overtime, star performances, and more — contributes to a game's score, right from Settings, per league. Reset to the default anytime.

No accounts, no sign-up. Just a fast way to find out which games are worth your time. Supported by a small banner ad — a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is available for anyone who'd rather not see it.
