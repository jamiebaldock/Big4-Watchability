Privacy Policy for Big4 Watchability

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Big4 Watchability ("the app") does not require an account and does not
collect your name, email address, or other identifying information. It
does show ads and use push notifications, both described below.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
- No account creation, sign-in, or sign-up of any kind.
- No collection of your name, email address, contacts, or photos.
- No data is sold to third parties.

ADVERTISING
The app shows banner ads via Google AdMob to support development. AdMob
may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID) and general
usage data to serve and measure ads, and may use this data across other
apps you use, per Google's own advertising policies. A one-time in-app
purchase ("Remove Ads") is available to disable ads entirely. See
Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads for how
AdMob handles this data.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
If you enable Alerts (starting-soon or close-game notifications) in
Settings, the app registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging
(a Google service) so it can be sent a notification. This registration
uses a device token, not your name or email. You can disable Alerts at
any time in Settings, which stops new notifications; see Google's policy
at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy for how Firebase handles
this data.

WHAT'S STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
The app saves a small set of preferences locally on your device only,
using Android's standard app-preferences storage: your chosen
watchability-rating weights, which leagues and teams/players you've
favorited, your starred games, and which league you last viewed.

None of this ever leaves your device except as described above (ads,
push notifications). Uninstalling the app deletes it.

NETWORK REQUESTS
To show you game schedules, scores, and previews, the app makes standard
internet requests to:
- The app's own backend server, to fetch game data and pre-written game
  previews across its supported leagues.
- ESPN's public content servers, to load team logo images.
- YouTube, to search for and play official highlight videos.

As with any internet request, your device's IP address is inherently
visible to these servers as part of normal network communication - the
same as visiting any website. The app's own backend does not log, store,
or associate requests with any personal profile.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Ad personalization for users known to be under 13 is restricted per
Google Play's Families policy where applicable.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a
revised "Last updated" date.
